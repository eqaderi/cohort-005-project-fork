# A Refund Request decision is final, and a purchase gets only one

Once an admin or the course's instructor Approves or Declines a Refund Request, the decision cannot be changed, and the learner cannot make another Refund Request on that purchase — even if the Refund Window is still open. A decision has effects that are hard to take back (a Refund is owed, access changes, the learner is emailed), and allowing repeat requests would let a learner keep asking until someone says yes. We accept that a mistaken click cannot be undone and that a declined learner has no appeal.

## Considered Options

- **Allow a new request after a Decline, while the window is open.** Kinder to a learner who gave a weak reason the first time, but turns every Decline into "not yet" and invites asking until approved. Rejected for that reason.
- **Let an admin reopen or reverse a decision.** Fixes honest mistakes, but needs extra states (reopened, cancelled Refund) and a way to take back emails and access changes already made. Rejected as not worth the complexity for a hand-reviewed, low-volume process.
